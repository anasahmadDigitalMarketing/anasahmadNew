A Quick Intro

A simple spot to show who I am and why I focus on ads that bring people through the door, not just clicks.

Hi, I'm Anas

I'm a freelance Meta Ads specialist based in India, focused on one corner of the market: businesses where the goal isn't a click, it's a person walking through the door or signing up for a batch.

Over the years I've managed campaigns for coaching institutes, schools, colleges, gyms, and health centres — accounts where "engagement" doesn't pay the bills, but enrollments and memberships do. I bring that same lens to every new account: what's the real action worth paying for, and how do we get more of it for less.

No jargon-heavy reports, no vanity metrics — just clear weekly numbers and a plan for what changes next.

How a new account starts

The first 30 days

The same five steps for every account, in the same order — so you always know what stage your campaigns are at.

01

Audit

Review account structure, pixel health, and the last 90 days of data to see what's actually been happening.

02

Plan

Map the funnel — awareness, lead, demo or trial, enrollment or membership — and set targets for each stage.

03

Launch

Build campaigns, ad sets, and creative variants, structured for clean testing from day one.

04

Optimize

Weekly budget shifts based on cost-per-result — the metric your business runs on, not vanity metrics.

05

Report
